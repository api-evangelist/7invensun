# 7Invensun Technology

> Beijing 7invensun Technology Co., Ltd. (北京七鑫易维信息技术有限公司) is a Chinese eye-tracking
> company founded in 2009. It builds eye-tracking hardware, gaze-estimation algorithms and
> device SDKs: the aSee family of glasses-style, desktop and high-speed research eye trackers,
> aSee VR modules embedded in XR headsets, an aSee Mobile developer accessory, and
> eye-controlled communication aids for people with ALS, high paraplegia and cerebral palsy.
> As of this profile 7Invensun publishes no public web API, no OpenAPI/AsyncAPI/GraphQL
> contract, and no developer portal. Its developer surface is a native device SDK (aSeeVR
> UserSDK and related C/C++ and mobile libraries) obtained from the company's own download
> center or under an OEM agreement.

## Products

- aSee Glasses / aSee Glasses Elite — glasses-style wearable eye trackers for research.
- aSee Pro / aSee Pro Plus — desktop and high-speed eye trackers for scientific research.
- aSee A series — portable eye trackers.
- aSee VR — eye-tracking modules for VR/XR headsets (foveated rendering, gaze interaction).
- aSee Mobile — phone/tablet eye-tracking accessory with a developer edition SDK.
- Eye-controlled communication aids (aSee AIO lineage) for motor-impaired users.

## Solutions

- Automotive driver monitoring (DMS) and in-cabin gaze interaction.
- Spatial computing / XR: foveated rendering, gaze interaction, identity and authentication.
- Phone and tablet gaze control: gaze unlock, gaze focus, gaze page-turn.
- Safety monitoring for eldercare and critical-illness settings.

## API surface

- No OpenAPI, AsyncAPI, GraphQL SDL, Postman collection or JSON Schema is published.
- No developer portal: `developer.7invensun.com`, `docs.7invensun.com` and `open.7invensun.com`
  do not resolve.
- `https://api.7invensun.com/` exists but answers HTTP 401 ("请登录" / please log in) on every
  path probed, including its root. No public reference for it is published.
- No first-party client library is published on npm, PyPI, NuGet, Maven Central, RubyGems,
  crates.io or pkg.go.dev.
